01Our Story

How It All Began

Once upon a time, Brooks traveled long and far down to the desolate desert of Arizona to test himself against the infamous riddles of Allison Lowe. Whoever was able to answer these riddles won her hand in marriage and her dowry of 100 stuffed chickens. Eager to try his luck, Brooks waded through the hoi polloi found on a commercial airline flight, lulling babies to sleep with his song and saving downed passengers.

Once the plane touched down, he eagerly rushed down into the dungeons of car rentals, only to discover that his car had been stolen by a woman and child pretending to be innocent. Luckily, he was able to woo the big corporate monster that was the parking lot attendant to get a new car. He rushed to pick flowers from the banks of the overpass and made his way to Allison's floral fortress.

She waited for him in a beautiful, flowery dress, surrounded by citrus trees. She was guarded by two fierce little, lazy dogs and a tortoise. Brooks barely made it out alive. He took her hand and led her to the chariot and then down to the Mediterranean market, where they were met with dead rabbits, unsolicited belly dancers, and a bust of Hermes. They were so excited that they barely ate and talked at the same time, neither person hearing a word of the other. It was love.

The rest was as predictable as the beginning, representing the average American love story. A few months went by, the wagon was packed and hauled by the finest team in Arizona to the great state of Montana where they bought a historic 1900s house and planned for many babies and large land purchases.

03Orthodox Wedding

What is an Orthodox Christian Wedding?

We recognize that the Orthodox Church may be unfamiliar to many of our guests. Orthodox Christianity is the ancient Christian faith, faithfully preserved and handed down through the centuries since the time of Christ and His Apostles. In the sacrament of marriage, a man and a woman are given the possibility to become one spirit and one flesh in a way which no human love can provide by itself. For those who are curious to learn more, we have included a few resources that may help explain the beauty and meaning of what you will see during the wedding service.
